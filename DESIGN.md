# DESIGN.md — Lemaiyans Travels Design Direction

## Product & Brand Identity
- **Entity**: Lemaiyans Travels Limited
- **Founded**: 2014, Eldoret, Kenya
- **Motto**: "Aviation is not just our business; it is the core of our existence."
- **Core Value Proposition**: Licensed IATA/KATA/TRA travel agency providing dependable air ticketing, worldwide accommodation, visa advisory, and ground transport for organizations and private travelers.
- **Audience**: Corporate travel managers, NGO logistical leads, government delegations, and individual international/domestic travelers.

## Liveliness Dials
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)** — Professional, trustworthy corporate travel authority with authentic warmth; avoids cold enterprise sterile defaults as well as flashy agency gimmicks.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Structured with Deliberate Variety)** — Varied section compositions built around specific content needs (e.g. 2-column feature leads, credential registers, and interactive map desks) rather than monotonous copy-paste 3-column card grids.
- **MOTION: 1 (Calm & Purposeful)** — Subtle hover states, smooth drawer transitions, and responsive feedback; avoids gratuitous scroll-triggered fade/scale animations on every element.

## Color Palette & Purpose
- **Primary Accent**: Crimson `#9B1D20` (Warmth, Kenyan identity, aviation energy). Used intentionally on primary calls to action, active indicators, and brand crest.
- **Deep Tone**: Burgundy `#7D171A` (Hover states and authoritative emphasis).
- **Executive Dark**: Navy `#131722` (Hero backdrops and footer base, providing grounding and contrast for photography).
- **Warm Neutral**: `#FAF9F6` (Page canvas; provides gentle organic contrast against pure white cards).
- **Surface White**: `#FFFFFF` (Card bodies and form containers).
- **Contrast Rule**: Text on white must always meet WCAG AA (>= 4.5:1, e.g. `#1F2532` or `#475569`). Icons on dark backgrounds use accessible high-contrast tints (`#FB7185` or `#FFFFFF`).

## Typography
- **Universal Brand Typeface**: `Inter` — Clean, modern, reliable sans-serif requested and explicitly confirmed by the owner for all display titles, headings, and body copy.
- **Sans-Serif Fallback Chain**: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif` — Prevents serif/Times New Roman defaults.

## Components & Craftsmanship
- **Interactive Controls**: Every button and link must have clear keyboard `:focus-visible` styling and minimum 44px tap target heights on mobile devices.
- **Truth in Content**: Verified credentials (IATA, KATA, TRA, AGPO) and real office contact info (Nandi Arcade, Eldoret) take precedence over generic AI claims or template testimonials.
