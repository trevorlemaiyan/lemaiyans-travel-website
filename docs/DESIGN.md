# Lemaiyans Travels — UI direction

## Brand

- **Company**: Lemaiyans Travels Limited, founded in 2014 and based in Eldoret, Kenya.
- **Audience**: Corporate travel managers, NGO logistics teams, government delegations, and individual travelers.
- **Voice**: Capable, direct, welcoming, and grounded in what the travel desk can actually arrange.

## Visual reference

Use the Quantum² landing page as a reference for its variable Figtree typography, concise hierarchy, floating pill navigation, generous whitespace, precise cards, and restrained motion. Apply those patterns to Lemaiyans’ multi-page content and keep the company’s established palette.

## Color palette

- **Primary crimson**: `#9B1D20` for primary actions, active states, and small brand accents.
- **Burgundy**: `#7D171A` for hover states and deeper emphasis.
- **Executive navy**: `#151922` for navigation, footer, and selected dark panels.
- **Warm canvas**: `#FAF9F6` for the main page background.
- **White surface**: `#FFFFFF` for cards and forms.
- **Soft border**: `#E8E7E3` for separators and card outlines.
- **Body ink**: `#1F2532` with slate neutrals for supporting copy.
- **Cyan is not a brand accent**; retain it only when required by an existing asset.

## Typography and layout

- Use the Figtree variable font for headings, navigation, and body copy, with a system sans-serif fallback.
- Keep display headlines large, compact, and carefully line-broken. Use readable body measures and relaxed line height.
- Use rounded pill navigation and primary buttons, fine borders, generous spacing, and editorial image crops.
- Use responsive document flow instead of fixed page canvases. Keep text and visuals in separate layout regions where that avoids collisions.
- Location copy should establish Eldoret as the office base and make clear that the team serves clients worldwide.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Keep keyboard focus visible, touch targets comfortable, and color contrast readable.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences. Use small, purposeful transitions rather than decorative motion on every section.
- Preserve semantic headings, descriptive image text, and clear link labels.

## Content integrity

- Describe credentials, service availability, and booking outcomes precisely; avoid guarantees the company cannot control.
- Use verified office details and current supporting documents for licensing and membership claims.
- Tour destinations are inspiration, not fixed packages; confirm routes, availability, terms, and pricing directly with travelers.
